# Data availability

Compound-level data are not included in this public release.

The withheld materials include molecular structures and SMILES, activity records, RRCS-selected receptor structures, Glide docking files, EquiScore representations, processed feature matrices, trained models, out-of-fold predictions, and candidate-level prediction results.

Some source records originated from Cortellis and may be subject to third-party licensing restrictions. Access to nonrestricted materials may be considered upon reasonable request, subject to institutional approval and applicable data-use agreements.
